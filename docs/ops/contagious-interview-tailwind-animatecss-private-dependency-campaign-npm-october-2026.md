**(6) DEFENDER READ:** (a) `--ignore-scripts` is not protection against require-time execution — audit `tailwind.config.js` plugin lists and every `require` of a scope your org has no reason to trust; (b) an `.npmrc` with an auth token inside a *take-home assessment repo* is the flag — legitimate interviews never need you to authenticate to a stranger's private scope; (c) `npm install` succeeding on a private scoped dependency you cannot look up at `registry.npmjs.org` is itself the anomaly — report it; (d) candidate/dev-facing campaigns monetize *people*, so IR scope includes the individual machine, not just CI.
